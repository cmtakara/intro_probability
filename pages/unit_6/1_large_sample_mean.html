Large Sample Estimation of a Population Mean

Confidence Intervals

Conditions that must be met

  • sample is randomly selected from population
  • The population distribution must be a normal distribution
  • The population standard deviation must be know

The confidence interval gives us a range for the estimated population mean based on the sample mean.

Formula

CI = Xbar +/- z * (pop std dev/sqrt(n))

Estimating when you don't have population standard deviation

SE = standard error = sample standard deviation/sqrt(n)

n is the number of samples
